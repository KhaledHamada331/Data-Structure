Binary Search Tree (BST)

In a BST, for every node, the values in the left subtree are smaller, and the values in the right subtree are larger. This structure allows for fast searching, insertion, and deletion.

Operations Logic:

  • Insert: Compare with root, go left if smaller, right if larger.
  • Delete:
    1. Leaf: Just remove.
    2. One Child: Replace with child.
    3. Two Children: Replace with In-order Successor.
